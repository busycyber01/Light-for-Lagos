Why this exists

Light shouldn't be a luxury.

Across Lagos, prepaid meters run dry at the worst possible moments — mid-exam-prep, mid-business, mid-week. Light for Lagos exists to close that gap for households who need a bridge, one free token at a time.

This is a direct, no-repayment gesture — funded and distributed transparently, with every token traceable to a verified meter and address before it's sent.

Before you apply

Who this is for.

  • ✓ You have a working prepaid meter registered to your name or household.
  • ✓ You live within one of Lagos State's Local Government Areas.
  • ✓ One application per household — this keeps the program fair and stretches it further.
  • ✓ You're comfortable being contacted by phone or email to confirm your details.
